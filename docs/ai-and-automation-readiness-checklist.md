# AI and Automation Readiness Checklist

Use this checklist to assess one process at a time.

- [ ] Write down the problem, the people affected, and what a better outcome would look like.
- [ ] Map the current steps, decisions, exceptions, and handoffs.
- [ ] Identify the process owner and the people who will use and support the solution.
- [ ] Record a baseline, such as time spent, rework, or errors.
- [ ] List the data sources, check quality and permissions, and identify sensitive information.
- [ ] Decide which steps can use fixed rules and which might benefit from AI.
- [ ] Define when a person should review, correct, or override a result.
- [ ] Pick one bounded pilot and agree on how to measure success and handle failures.
- [ ] Plan for access control, testing, documentation, release approval, and ongoing support.
- [ ] Review the pilot results with users and decide whether to improve, expand, or stop.

If ownership, data access, or a measure of success is unclear, address that gap before starting a pilot.
